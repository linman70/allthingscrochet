How the estimate works

We scale the swatch's weight by the area of your project, then use the yarn label's yards-per-gram ratio. Your extra allowance is included in the result.

Keep in mind: stitch patterns, fringe, shaping, gauge changes, and three-dimensional pieces can change actual yarn use. This tool is best for flat projects, like scarves and blankets.

FREE PATTERN · PETS

Little Kitty Crochet Ball

A small, soft ball for supervised play — no plastic eyes, buttons, or bells.

Easy US crochet terms Approx. 2.5 in / 6 cm About 30–60 min

Supplies

  • Approx. 20–30 yards of sturdy washable worsted cotton yarn
  • 3.5 mm hook (or size creating a tight fabric), stitch marker
  • Tapestry needle; small amount of fiberfill or washable fabric scraps

Stitches

MR = magic ring · sc = single crochet · inc = 2 sc in same stitch · dec = invisible decrease.

Instructions

  1. R1: 6 sc into MR. (6)
  2. R2: Inc in every stitch. (12)
  3. R3: [1 sc, inc] × 6. (18)
  4. R4: [2 sc, inc] × 6. (24)
  5. R5–R9: Sc in each stitch around. (24 each)
  6. R10: [2 sc, dec] × 6. (18)
  7. R11: [1 sc, dec] × 6. (12) Lightly stuff now.
  8. R12: Dec × 6. (6) Fasten off with a long tail. Thread through the remaining front loops, pull closed, then bury and secure the tail inside.

Finishing & safety

Inspect every seam and weave in ends firmly. Always supervise pets and remove the toy immediately if it frays or opens. Some pets swallow string or stuffing; never leave yarn toys unattended, and skip this project for pets that chew and ingest fabric. Wash and dry it thoroughly as needed.

Original pattern by All Things Crochet. Photos are illustrative examples of other hand-crocheted pet-friendly toy ideas, not an exact sample of this ball.

♡ PET-SAFE DETAILS

Simple, embroidered-free designs with secure seams are safer than toys with detachable decorations — but supervision is always essential.

FREE PATTERN · ACCESSORIES

Cozy Weekend Scarf

A soft, textured rectangle that is easy to customize for any length.

Confident beginner US crochet terms Approx. 7 × 60 in 3–5 hours

Supplies

  • Approx. 350–425 yards of medium (weight 4) yarn
  • 5.5 mm (I/9) crochet hook, or a hook that achieves your preferred drape
  • Tapestry needle, scissors, measuring tape

Stitches

ch = chain · hdc = half double crochet · BLO = back loop only · st = stitch. A turning chain does not count as a stitch in this pattern.

Instructions

  1. Foundation: Ch 24 loosely.
  2. Row 1: Hdc in 3rd ch from hook and in each ch across. (22 hdc)
  3. Row 2: Ch 2, turn. Hdc in BLO of each st across. (22 hdc)
  4. Rows 3 onward: Repeat Row 2 until the scarf measures approximately 60 in (152 cm), or your chosen length.
  5. Last row: Ch 1, fasten off. Weave in all yarn ends securely.
  6. Optional fringe: Cut 10–12 in strands, fold them in groups, and loop them evenly through each short end. Trim to match.

Make it yours

For a wider scarf, add chains in the foundation; each extra chain adds one hdc to every row. For a longer scarf, repeat Row 2. Check the drape after a few rows, and use the yardage calculator above to estimate yarn for your chosen dimensions.

Original scarf pattern by All Things Crochet. Photos show other crocheted scarves for design inspiration, rather than the exact pattern sample.

✳ NO-STRESS TIP

Measure your first few rows before committing to the full scarf. A small gauge change can make a big difference in the final width.
